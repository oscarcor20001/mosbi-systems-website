## Featured Product

# Machine Identifier AI™

An AI-powered platform designed to help identify industrial machinery and equipment quickly and accurately.

### Core Capabilities

- **Equipment recognition using AI:** Instant identification of complex industrial models, turbines, valves, and gearboxes.
- **Visual asset identification:** Point-and-shoot camera identification with no tags, barcodes, or manual lookups required.
- **Industrial equipment documentation:** Immediate digital linking to OEM schematics, torque specs, and historical maintenance logs.
- **Maintenance support workflows:** Context-aware checklists and step-by-step diagnostic procedures for frontline technicians.
- **Machine data collection and organization:** Automated aggregation of plant-floor operating conditions into unified digital twins.
- **Field technician productivity enhancement:** Eliminate wasted transit and manual code-matching time for technicians on the floor.

### Measurable ROI Benefits

- ✅ **Faster equipment identification** (Slash verification time by up to 90%)
- ✅ **Reduced manual documentation effort** (Eliminate paper binders & manual errors)
- ✅ **Faster troubleshooting & maintenance** (Accelerate MTTR with optical schematics)
- ✅ **Improved knowledge transfer** (Empower junior technicians on day one)
- ✅ **Streamlined operational workflows** (Seamless ERP/CMMS synchronization)
